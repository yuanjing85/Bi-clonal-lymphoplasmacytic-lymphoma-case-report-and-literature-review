# Bi-clonal-lymphoplasmacytic-lymphoma-case-report-and-literature-review
Bi-clonal lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma: case report and literature review
Abstract
Lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma (LPL) with bi-clonal gammopathy is clinically extremely rare. We present a patient of LPL, whose serum electrophoresis reports revealed bi-clonal IgM-κ and IgA-κ. MYD88 L265P mutation (14.5%) and CXCR4 R334 mutation (3.76%) were detected by next-generation sequencing assays. After a review of previous medical literature, we identified 14 cases of LPL with biclonality. The median age was 72.5 years (range, 45~86 years) with male predominance (11 men, only 3 women). The most common symptoms at diagnosis are fatigue and anorexia. Compared with Waldenstrom macroglobulinemia (WM), patients with non-IgM LPL showed similar clinical and pathologic features. Bendamustine-rituximab regimen as induction therapy, subsequently ibrutinib as maintenance therapy, offers a valid therapeutic option. 
Key words
Lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma; biclonality; MYD88; CXCR4; ibrutinib
